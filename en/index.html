The studio

Forgea Games is an independent studio. We love games you play for a little while every day, worlds that keep going while you are away, and pixel art that cares about every detail.

  • Real pixel art

    Every scene is drawn at 480×270 and scaled up without smoothing, so every pixel shows.

  • At your pace

    Real-time games: drop in for a bit, make your call and get on with your day.

  • In your language

    Our games ship in Spanish, English and Portuguese from day one.
