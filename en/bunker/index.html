The game day follows your clock. High sun, and the solar panels at full power.

Build and keep a bunker alive in real time through the end of the world.

Dig your bunker, organise your people and decide who gets in. Everything runs in real time: works take real minutes or hours, crops grow while you are away and catastrophes arrive with a warning on the radio.

What you will do

  • Dig and build

    Bedroom, kitchen, hydroponics, generator, radio, infirmary, workshop, classroom and more. Every room has its own size and levels.

  • Head outside

    Farm, raise chickens, gather water and power, and defend the fence.

  • Decide who gets in

    Visitors show up, and some of them lie. Trade with the merchant and send expeditions to the gas station, the hospital or the mine.

  • Weather the catastrophes

    Meteor showers, sandstorms, blizzards, wolves and raids. The radio warns you first, and then there is repair work to do.

  • Uncover the map

    With the Map room you find the town, the ruined city, the military base, the neighbours and the raiders' camp.

  • Up to 3 bunkers

    Each with its own biome and name, and all three keep running while you play another.

No ads. Optional gold purchases to finish works instantly.
