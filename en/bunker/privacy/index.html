Last Prepper privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Last Prepper is a game by Forgea Games. This page explains what data the game uses and what we do with it. If you have any question, write to us at contacto@forgeagames.com.

What stays on your device

Your games, the game account (gold, purchases, settings and language) and your progress are saved on your device. We never ask for your e-mail, a password or any personal details.

Friends and visits

So you can add friends, visit their bunkers, leave them gifts and lend them people, the game connects to a server of ours. There it creates an anonymous account, tied to a random identifier of your device, and keeps only what is needed for that to work:

  • your player name (the one from Game Center or Google Play Games, or the one you choose) and your friend code;
  • your friends and friend requests;
  • a snapshot of your bunker (rooms, people, buildings and biome) so your friends can visit it;
  • the gifts, likes, notes and people lent between friends. Notes are ready-made phrases: there is no chat and no free text;
  • the reports you make about other players.

Your name, your level and the snapshot of your bunker can be seen by your friends and by anyone who searches your name to add you.

Cloud save

If you turn it on in Settings, your games are copied to the cloud of your platform (Google Play Games on Android, iCloud on iOS) with your account there. We never receive or read them.

Achievements

If you are signed in to Game Center or Google Play Games, the achievements you unlock are saved to your account on that platform.

Purchases

Gold and the starter pack are bought through Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card or payment details: the store only tells us that a purchase was made.

Server time

When the game opens it asks a public server for the time over the internet (one HTTPS request with no data of yours), so game timers stay real even if the phone clock changes.

Notifications

Notifications about finished works, catastrophes and the merchant are local: your phone schedules them, no server does. They can be turned off in Settings.

What we do not do

No ads, no tracking or usage analytics, and we do not sell or share your data.

How long we keep it and how to delete it

What is on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. Friends and visits data stays on our server while you use those features. If you want us to delete it, write to contacto@forgeagames.com with your friend code (it starts with BUNK-) and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

We ask nobody for personal details, children included. There is no chat or free text between players.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page, with its date.
